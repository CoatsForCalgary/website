Who your coat helps, and why it matters.

Every coat goes to the Calgary Drop-In Centre. It hands them out to its own guests and to families in need. It also shares them with more than 100 other social service groups across Calgary, so your coat can reach people the Drop-In never meets.

For more than 50 years the Drop-In has supported people who are homeless or at risk of losing their home. It is open around the clock, every day of the year.

Why coats

Calgary winters are long and can drop below −30. Many of the people the Drop-In serves walk everywhere: to work, to appointments, to a bed for the night. Coats wear out, get lost or stolen, and the need starts again every fall.

What to bring

We take

  • Any coat, any size, in any condition. Torn and worn are fine.
  • Mitts, gloves and scarves

No need to wash anything first.

What happens to it

  • Everything is cleaned.
  • Damaged items are repaired, then given out.
  • Anything beyond repair is sold to a manufacturer and recycled.
