Who your coat helps, and why it matters.

Every coat is donated to the Free Goods Program by the Calgary Drop-In Centre. The coats are then dispersed to their clients or shared with over 130 agencies and outreach groups. Your coat reaches the people who need it most.

Why coats?

Calgary winters are long and can drop below −30°C. Many of the clients the Calgary Drop-In serves walk everywhere: to work, to appointments, or to a bed for the night. Coats can wear out, get lost or stolen, and the need starts again every cold season.

What to bring

We take

  • Clean, gently used or new coats. All sizes, adult and children.
  • Warm winter items, including mitts, gloves, scarves and snow pants

What happens to it

  • Coats are dispersed to those who need them most within the Calgary Drop-In Centre.
  • Requests from outside agencies and outreach groups are fulfilled.
  • Anything beyond repair is sold for materials and recycled.
